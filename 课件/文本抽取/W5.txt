[p20] 2.1 Business Cycle | • 4 most commonly quoted causes of business cycle: | 1. Multiplier and accelerator | 2. Time-lags | 3. Stock-building | 4. Mass psychology
[p21] 2.1.1 Multiplier and Accelerator | • An increase in investment (e.g., new factories set up)  | creates new consumer demand from the workers  | hired. | • Workers spend their wages > creates more demand  | (_______________ multiplier). | • As extra consumer expenditure trickles through the  | economy, growth picks up, generating demand for  | even more investment.
[p22] 2.1.1 Multiplier and Accelerator | • Eventually, capital becomes fully utilized and economy  | over-heats. | • Expansion sharply halted, throwing whole process into  | reverse. | • Investment falls off, jobs are lost, and multiplier and  | accelerator go into reverse > recession.
[p23] 2.1.2 Time Lags | • Delays between economic decisions and their  | implementation make cyclical fluctuations more  | extreme. | • _______________ – excessive interest rates cuts to fix  | slow economy .  | • ________________ – businesses make plans based on  | current conditions but regulations/money supplies  | changing today will only hit their profits much later.
[p24] 2.1.3 Stock-building | • Stock-building produces sudden bursts of demand as  | industries adjust their stocks/inventory during the  | business cycle.  | • During recessions, financially hard-pressed  | manufacturers stop ordering new inventory,  | intensifying downturn in demand for sea transport.  | • When economy recovers, sudden rush to rebuild  | inventory > sudden burst in commodity demand.
[p25] 2.1.4 Mass Psychology | • If they act in an imitative manner, a particular trend  | will build up where they can affect whole economic  | system.  | • Periods of optimism or pessimism become self- | fulfilling through medium of stock exchanges, financial  | booms and behaviour of investors.
[p26] 2.2 Trade Development Cycle | • Regional development cycles determine the medium  | to long-term trends in seaborne trade.
[p27] 2.2 Trade Development Cycle | • Two reasons why, over long periods, trade growth of  | individual regions will probably change.  | a. Economic structure changes over time. | b. Ability of local resources of food and raw materials  | to meet local demand.
