[p31] 2.1.4 Contract of Affreightment (COA) | • S/O able to plan the use of his ship in the most  | efficient manner. |   | → switch cargo between vessels to give the best  | possible operating pattern. |   | → arrange backhaul cargoes to improve utilisation.  | • Most COA business specialises in iron ore and coal with  | major customers the steel mills in the Far East.
[p32] 2.1.5 Time Charter | • An agreement between shipowner and charterer to  | hire the ship, complete with crew, operated by the  | shipowner under instructions from the charterer, for a  | fee per day, month or year.  | • Shipowner pays capital and operating expenses (crew,  | maintenance and repair) | • Charterer pays voyage costs (bunkers, port charges  | and canal dues) and cargo handling costs.
[p33] 2.1.5 Time Charter | • Shipowner must state the vessel’s speed, fuel  | consumption and cargo capacity. | • Charterparty will set out conditions under which the  | vessel is regard as ‘off-hire’. |   | E.g., emergency repairs or when charterer fails to pay  | charter hire. | • Long term charters deals with (i) adjustment to hire in  | event of lay up and (ii) termination if shipowner fails to  | run ship effectively.

[p73] 3. Market Cycle
[p74] 3. What is a market cycle? | • An interval of time during which one sequence of a  | regularly recurring sequence of events is completed.
[p75] 3. What is a market cycle? | • Market cycles pervade the shipping industry. | • Waves of shipping cycles ripple through the financial  | lives of shipowners.
[p76] 
[p77] 3. What is a market cycle? | • Cycles occur in many industries, not unique to  | shipping. | • Consequence of market mechanism. | • Market adjusting supply to meet demand by regulating  | cashflow. | • Cycles create environment in which weak shipping  | companies are forced out, leaving the strong to survive  | and prosper.
[p78] 3.1 Shipping Market Cycle | • Shipping cycle – mechanism that coordinates supply  | and demand in shipping market > financial switchbox  | that regulate investment. | • Complete cycle has ____ stages. | • Shipping cycles are episodic – no firm rules about  | timing and extent.  | • No simple formula for predicting shape of next cycle.
[p79] 3.2 Seasonal Cycles | • Occur widely in shipping causing fluctuations in freight  | rates during to seasonal patterns of demand for sea  | transport. | (i) Surge in grain movements during late September and  | October as North American harvests is shipped.  | (ii) In reefer trade, movement of fresh fruit during the  | harvest in the Northern Hemisphere. | (iii) Stocking up of oil for periods of peak demand in the  | winter.
