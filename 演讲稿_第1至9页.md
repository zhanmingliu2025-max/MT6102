## 第 1 页 封面（约 80 词，0:35）

Good evening, members of the board. We are Group X, and you asked us to put USD 250 million to work in shipping. Our answer in one sentence: skip the peak, buy value, lock income. We buy five secondhand ships, fix three of them on period charters, trade two in the spot market, and keep a small cash reserve. Every price and every rate you will see tonight is a real transaction taken from the Clarksons Shipping Intelligence Network.

**要点**：董事会口吻开场；一句话结论；五艘船、三锁两现货、少量现金；所有数字来自 Clarksons SIN 的真实成交。

## 第 3 页 分配、理由、数据（约 185 词，1:20）

**EN**
This one table is the whole project. Read it left to right: how much, why, and the data.

Two MR product tankers, 100 million, forty percent. MR is the only segment priced at replacement cost: a five-year-old ship at 0.99 times newbuild in October. Products trade grows 6.3 percent next year, and spot earnings of 53,741 dollars a day sit above the one-year charter, so one ship is fixed and one stays open.

One Aframax, 75 million, thirty percent. The smallest orderbook of any segment, 8.4 percent, 62 ships on order against 223 over twenty years old. A three-year charter at 40,000 a day was fixed on 31 August, so that income is certain through the delivery wave.

Two Kamsarmaxes, 70 million, twenty-eight percent. Dry bulk is in recovery, not at a peak: prices only nine percent above newbuild, and Guinea bauxite exports up 29 percent.

Five million in cash: five months of operating costs and dry powder for the next dip.

Sixty percent of ship-days are fixed. That gives 17.9 percent in the worst case, 19.9 in the base case, 23.7 in the best.

**要点**：四行各讲"分多少、为什么、数据"；MR 0.99 倍重置成本、成品油 +6.3%、即期 53,741；Aframax 订单簿 8.4%、62 对 223、三年 40,000；Kamsarmax 复苏未过热、溢价 9%、铝土矿 +29%；现金 5 个月 OPEX；合计 70/28/2，锁定 60%，17.9 / 19.9 / 23.7。

## 第 5 页 为什么买二手（约 115 词，0:50）

**EN**
The brief allows us to buy or to charter, so we costed three routes. Chartering in locks a cost at the top of the cycle: a chartered-in MR costs 30,000 dollars a day and earns 27,000 in our base case, so only the bull case pays, and there is no asset to sell afterwards. Newbuilding cannot deliver inside the window: yards are full to 2029, and the first ships would arrive in the middle of the 2027 to 2028 delivery wave. That leaves secondhand. The chart shows five-year-old prices against newbuild prices. Our segments sit at or just above the line. Suezmax sits far above it, and that is the one segment we refuse.

**要点**：三条路都算过；租入在山顶锁成本，30,000 对 27,000；新造 2029 年才有船，正撞交付潮；二手里挑贴近重置成本的，Suezmax 远高于线上所以不买。

## 第 6 页 四条贸易（约 70 词，0:30）

Where do the ships go? The MRs carry clean products from Middle East and Indian refineries to East Africa and South East Asia, where longer hauls are adding tonne-miles. The Aframax is handed to an oil-major-class charterer for three years. One Kamsarmax carries bauxite from Guinea to China, the longest haul in dry bulk, on a twelve-month charter. **要点**：MR 中东与印度炼厂到东非、东南亚；Aframax 整租三年；Kamsarmax 一艘几内亚铝土矿到中国，一艘四季度粮食再转铝土矿与煤。

## 第 7 页 定价依据一：二手船价（约 100 词，0:45）

**EN**
Part two shows where every number comes from. First, purchase prices. Two Aframaxes of the same type and age, Southern Reverence and Pusaka Borneo, both sold at exactly 75 million this spring, so that price is firm. Our two Kamsarmaxes are benchmarked to two Japan-built sales on the same day, 10 August: Aquavita Aim at 38 million and Medi Positano at 32 million. The next line is the control. Presinge, a Chinese-built sister of the same age, sold one day later for 29.25 million. That 2.75 million gap is what the market charges for the port-fee insurance we are buying.

**要点**：两笔同型同龄 Aframax 都是 7,500 万；两艘 Kamsarmax 对标 8 月 10 日两笔日本造成交；对照组 Presinge 2,925 万，275 万价差就是港口费保险的价格。

## 第 8 页 定价依据二：期租租金（约 105 词，0:45）

## 第 9 页 定价依据三：即期运价（约 100 词，0:45）

**EN**
Third, freight rates, the item the brief asks for by name. Our spot ships trade coal and clean products, and both routes have fresh fixtures. A Kamsarmax was fixed on 10 September to carry 80,000 tonnes of coal from Dalrymple Bay in Australia to Vizag in India at 25.60 dollars a tonne. At September bunker prices that converts to about 27,400 dollars a day, above the 23,000 we assume. A second coal fixture to Korea was done the day before. On the products side, an MR took gasoline from the Baltic to the US Gulf at Worldscale 105. The last two lines are context only: Suezmax crude at Worldscale 210 and 388, two to three times normal. That is the war-premium market we deliberately stay out of. So we have three price families and seventeen transactions, all last done. Part three asks whether the fundamentals can hold these prices.

**要点**：运价是题目点名要的；煤炭 DBCT 到 Vizag 25.60 美元/吨，换算约 27,400/天高于基准 23,000；第二笔煤炭到韩国；MR 波罗的海到美湾 WS105；Suezmax WS210、WS388 只作对照，我们刻意避开；三类价格 17 笔成交全是 last done；链接到第 3 部分供需。

## 问答时第 1 至 9 页最可能被追问的三句话

1. 为什么只留 500 万现金：去年有组留 1 亿被老师追问；我们留 2%，正好是五个月营运成本，锁定租约每月还回 270 万。
2. 是不是买在高点：分船型答，Suezmax 在高点所以没买；MR 等于重置成本；Kamsarmax 溢价 9%，远低于 2007 年 Panamax 5 年船龄价比新船贵近一倍的那种倒挂。
3. 为什么不租入：租入 MR 一年付 30,000，现货基准只赚 27,000，熊市 22,950；只有牛市 53,741 才赚钱，而且没有船舶增值。
