## Synopsis

The dry bulk market has made a strong start to 2026, with earnings recently hitting unseasonal multi-year highs, building momentum going into the summer. Strength has been led by Capesizes, kicking on again after a robust 2H 2025, while improving ‘sub-Cape’ performance has broadened the rally. Here we put recent bulker market strength into context and weigh up the drivers.
