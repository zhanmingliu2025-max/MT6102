## Synopsis

Grain trade has delivered a bountiful harvest for dry bulk shipping so far in 2026, led by record shipments from major exporters in the Americas. Strong volumes on long-haul routes into Asia have supported tonne-mile demand growth and provided a tailwind for mid-sized bulkers in particular. However, there are a range of complexities to watch ahead, from geopolitics to climate patterns…
