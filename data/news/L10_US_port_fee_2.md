## Synopsis

The announcement of a one year 'truce' between the US and China eases some of the recent trade tensions and disruption for maritime. US tariffs on imports from China will be lowered, while both the US and China have agreed to suspend recently introduced port fees.

## 要点
**美中双方自 2025-10-30 起暂停港口费一年 → 2026 年 10 月底到期**；投资提案中对中国建造船的费率敞口应按"暂停但可能恢复"情景处理。
