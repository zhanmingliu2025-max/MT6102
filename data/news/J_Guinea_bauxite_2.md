## Synopsis

Bauxite exports from Guinea have continued to grow rapidly so far in 2025, providing growing demand for Capesize vessels which handle ~90% of shipments. Exports in 1H 2025 were up by 35% y-o-y to 93mt, with around 82% of this volume being shipped long-haul to China.
