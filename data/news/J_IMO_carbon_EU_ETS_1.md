# EU Review of the Emissions Trading Scheme (ETS)

- Keyword: EU ETS (IMO carbon/EU ETS)

## Synopsis

On 17th July 2026, the EU Commission published its review of the EU ETS, along with its proposals for future improvements to the regulation. EU countries and the European Parliament will now propose their own revisions and negotiate the final amendments, a process which is expected to take place over the next couple of years.
