# Newbuilding Investment Running ‘Hot’

- Keyword: newbuilding orders (newbuilding orders)
- Date: 2024-10-04
- Source: Clarksons Research SIN News - Sarah Holden
- Link: https://sin.clarksons.net/News/Article/204494
- Accessed: 2026-09-12

## Synopsis

The newbuilding market has seen a strong flow of activity in 2024 so far, with the volume of tonnage ordered up by 70% on the ten year run-rate. In terms of the value of investment, the trend has been even more acute, with 2024 already representing the fourth strongest year on record, exceeded only by the mid-2000s order boom.
