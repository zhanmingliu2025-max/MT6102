- Keyword: EU ETS (IMO carbon/EU ETS)

- Link: https://sin.clarksons.net/News/Article/211517

## Synopsis

Container markets have seen divergent trends in 2025 after firm gains in 2024, with freight rates weakening, whilst the charter market has shown resilience amid tight tonnage availability. Weaker underlying fundamentals and building tariff impacts suggest potential for softer container shipping markets ahead, albeit with major uncertainty and a range of scenarios possible.
