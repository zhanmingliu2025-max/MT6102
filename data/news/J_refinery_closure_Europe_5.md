## Synopsis

The average haul of global seaborne oil products trade has trended upwards over the last five years, increasing by an estimated 11% across 2018-22. However, gains in average haul have accelerated notably this year, largely on the back of Russia-related trade flow shifts. Here we take a closer look at the recent shifts in global products trade patterns.
