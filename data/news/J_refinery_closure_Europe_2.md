## Synopsis

The rapid increase in oil and gas production and exports in the United States has been one of the key tanker and gas carrier demand trends over the past decade, with the US on track to become the largest seaborne “energy” exporter this year. However, following a period of consistent growth, a more nuanced situation is developing with variation between cargoes emerging.
