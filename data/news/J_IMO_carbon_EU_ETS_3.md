## Synopsis

Last month, the IMO reached a landmark agreement on the next phase of regulation to drive shipping’s emissions reduction, amid targets for ‘net zero’ to be reached by 2050. Shipping is now the first sector to have an agreed global carbon pricing mechanism, and the deal marks a further ramp-up in the environmental regulatory landscape impacting the shipping industry.
