## Synopsis

Geopolitical events again dominated the agenda for shipping through first quarter 2026, with major disruption and complex market conditions. Alongside the unprecedented interruption to Middle East cargo flows, shipping markets (especially ‘energy’ segments) have been elevated (our cross-sector ClarkSea Index averaged $36,865/day in Q1), albeit with uncertainty “clouding” the outlook.
