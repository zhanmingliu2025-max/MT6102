# Container Port Congestion Back In The Spotlight

- Keyword: Red Sea Suez (Red Sea/Suez route risk)
- Date: 2024-07-04
- Source: Clarksons Research SIN News - Trevor Crowe
- Link: https://sin.clarksons.net/News/Article/201682
- Accessed: 2026-09-12

## Synopsis

Back in 2021/22, port congestion and broader logistical disruption were major features of the container shipping markets, helping drive freight rates (and shipper costs) and vessel charter rates to unprecedented levels. Having wound down across 2H-2022 &amp; 2023, container port congestion this year, following the onset of the disruption in the Red Sea, is having a notable impact once again.
