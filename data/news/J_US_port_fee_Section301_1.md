## Synopsis

Shipping has again it seems been at the “frontline” of geo-politics this week, with US port fees on Chinese related vessels commencing, rapid introduction of Chinese “counter measures” and heated discussions at IMO around the pace and direction of emissions reduction. In this week’s Analysis, we try to take stock after a “chaotic” week, aiming to provide a framework for assessing impacts.
